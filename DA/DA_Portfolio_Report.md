# Portfolio Project: Retail Strategy and Analytics
**Optimizing Store Performance through Customer Analytics and A/B Testing**

## Project Overview
This project analyzes transaction data from a major retail chain to gain insights into customer purchasing behaviour and evaluate the performance of trial store layouts. The goal is to provide data-driven recommendations for category management and strategic decision-making.

## Part 3: Experimentation Strategy (A/B Testing)
**Selecting Control Stores.** To evaluate the impact of a trial (started in Feb 2019) at Store 77, we needed a benchmark. We selected a "Control Store" that behaves similarly to Store 77 in the pre-trial period.

### Methodology
We used a composite score based on two metrics:
1.  **Correlation (0.5 weight):** Do sales trends move together?
    *   *Pearson Correlation* was calculated for `totSales` and `nCustomers`.
2.  **Magnitude Distance (0.5 weight):** Are the absolute sales numbers similar?
    *   *Normalized Euclidean Distance* (1 - standardized difference).

### Result: Store 77 vs. Store 233
Our algorithm identified **Store 233** as the perfect "Twin" for Store 77, with the highest match score (0.97).
